How to send your track

Two minutes on your phone. Pick the app your activity is in.

GarminKomootStrava AllTrailsCOROSSuunto Apple WatchPolarOther

Can’t do it right now? You can order first and send it later. See Send it after you order.

Garmin Connect Easiest: just a link

  1. In the Garmin Connect app, open the activity.
  2. Set its privacy to Everyone (tap the edit pencil or ⋯ at the top, then Privacy), and save.
  3. Tap Share → Copy link.

Then: paste the link in step 1 of the order form. We download the track for you.

Komoot Easiest: just a link

  1. In the Komoot app, tap Profile and open the tour.
  2. Tap Share → Copy link. This works even if the tour is private.

Then: paste the link in step 1 of the order form. We check it straight away.

Strava About 2 minutes

Strava doesn’t let anyone but you download your track, even when the activity is set to Everyone, and the Strava app can’t save a file. Your phone’s web browser can:

  1. In the Strava app, open the activity → Share → Copy link, and paste it here:
We’ll turn it into your download link.
  1. In Safari or Chrome (not the app), go to strava.com and log in.
  2. Paste the download link into the address bar and go. The GPX file downloads.

If the download link doesn’t work, open the activity on strava.com, switch your browser to the desktop site (Safari: page menu aA or ⋯ beside the address → Request Desktop Website; Chrome: ⋮ → Desktop site), then tap ⋯ → Export GPX.

Then: in step 1 of the order form, tap Upload file and pick it from Downloads.

Recorded on a Garmin, COROS or Suunto watch? Use that app instead, it’s quicker.

AllTrails About 2 minutes

AllTrails only lets you download your recordings from its website, so use your phone’s browser:

  1. Go to alltrails.com, log in, and switch to the desktop site (Safari: page menu aA or ⋯ → Request Desktop Website; Chrome: ⋮ → Desktop site).
  2. Tap your profile picture → Activities, and open the activity.
  3. Tap ⋯ above the title → Download route → GPX Track → OK.

Then: in step 1 of the order form, tap Upload file and pick it from Downloads.

COROS About 1 minute

  1. In the COROS app, go to Activities and open the activity.
  2. Tap ⋯ (top right) → Export Data → .GPX.
  3. Choose Save to Files (iPhone) or save it to your phone (Android).

Then: in step 1 of the order form, tap Upload file and pick the file.

Suunto About 1 minute

  1. In the Suunto app, go to your Calendar and open the workout.
  2. Tap ⋯ (top right) and export it as a FIT or GPX file.
  3. Choose Save to Files (iPhone) or save it to your phone (Android).

Then: in step 1 of the order form, tap Upload file and pick the file.

Apple Watch About 2 minutes

Apple’s Fitness app can’t save your route as a file, so you need a small free helper app.

  1. Install WorkoutGPX (free) from the App Store and let it read your workouts.
  2. Find the workout and export it as a GPX.
  3. Choose Save to Files.

Then: in step 1 of the order form, tap Upload file and pick the file.

Does your watch sync to Strava? You can use the Strava steps instead.

Polar About 2 minutes

Polar’s app can share a picture but not a file, so use your phone’s browser:

  1. Go to flow.polar.com and log in (switch to the desktop site if the page looks cut down).
  2. Open Diary, then the session.
  3. Tap Export → GPX.

Then: in step 1 of the order form, tap Upload file and pick it from Downloads.

Any other app or watch

  1. Open the activity and look for Export, Download or Share file, often under ⋯.
  2. Save the file to your phone. GPX, FIT, TCX or KML all work, and so does a Garmin zip.

Then: in step 1 of the order form, tap Upload file and pick the file.

Only have a public link? Paste it in the order form and we’ll check whether we can use it.

Finding the file on your phone

When you tap Upload file, your phone opens its file picker. On iPhone tap Browse and look in Downloads (in iCloud Drive or On My iPhone). On Android it’s in Downloads.

Trip over several days?

Send every day. Links: paste one per line. Files: select them all at once. We join them into one piece, with a marker for each night’s stop.

Send it after you order

Tap Can’t do it now? Order now, send it later in step 1. After checkout, reply to your order email with the file attached (or a link). We start your 3D model when it arrives, and you approve it before we make anything.

No GPS file at all?

No problem. In step 1 tap No GPS file, pick a famous route or type the trail and your dates, and we’ll trace it. A screenshot of your map helps: reply to your order email with it.
